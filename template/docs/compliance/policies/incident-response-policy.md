# Incident Response Policy

Owner: Security lead
Last review: <date>

## Reporting channel
Email: security@<domain>. See `SECURITY.md`.

## Severities
- **Sev 1** — active breach or imminent harm; respond immediately, all hands
- **Sev 2** — control failure with potential impact; respond same business day
- **Sev 3** — minor anomaly; respond within 5 business days

## Process
1. Triage and classify within 4 hours of report
2. Contain and eradicate
3. Recover and verify
4. Post-incident review within 10 business days
5. Update controls and document in `docs/compliance/evidence/`

## Required records
- Initial report (sanitized)
- Timeline
- Root cause
- Remediation
- Lessons learned
- Control changes adopted
