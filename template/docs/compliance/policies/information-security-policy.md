# Information Security Policy

Owner: <CTO / CISO>
Review cadence: Annually, or on material change
Last review: <date>
Approved by: <name>

## Purpose
Establish the principles by which <organization> protects information assets.

## Scope
Applies to all employees, contractors, and systems processing organization or customer data.

## Principles
1. Information is classified per `docs/compliance/policies/data-classification-policy.md`.
2. Access is granted on least-privilege, need-to-know basis (A.5.15, A.8.2).
3. Risks are identified, assessed, and treated per `risk-management-policy.md`.
4. Incidents are reported per `incident-response-policy.md`.
5. Suppliers handling our data are reviewed per `supplier-security-policy.md`.

## Roles and responsibilities
- **Executive sponsor**: <name> — owns the ISMS
- **Security lead**: <name> — operates day-to-day
- **All staff**: comply with this policy and report violations

## Compliance and review
The ISMS is audited internally annually and by external auditors per certification cycle.
This policy is reviewed annually and updated when controls change materially.
